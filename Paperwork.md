E. Entity status — Micro Entity Certification (Form SB/15A)

Micro entity requires all of:

· Qualifies as a small entity (fewer than 500 employees, no assignment to a large entity);
· Gross income (calendar year preceding the fee year) below the USPTO's published limit (check the current-year figure — it moves with median household income);
· Not named as inventor on more than 4 previously filed US applications (excluding provisionals), and not named on more than 4 applications in the preceding calendar year;
· No obligation to assign to an entity that exceeds the income limit.

```
I certify that the applicant qualifies as a micro entity per 37 CFR 1.29:
Inventor name:    Lewis James Seals Jr
Signature:        /Lewis James Seals Jr/
Date:             [YYYY-MM-DD]
```

If any of those four conditions fails, use Small Entity (Form SB/09a) instead. Do not certify micro if uncertain — a later correction carries the higher fee plus a surcharge.
